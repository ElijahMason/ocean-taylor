Example briefs

Notes on the kind of work.

Four examples. They show the shape of a brief. They are not a client list.

01 — Example, consumer audio

Quiet Signal

A product launch told through reviews.

The brief is a good object and a plan that is too loud. The work is a short narrative, a press kit with a few claims and proof for each, and a social cadence that answers questions.

Paid media waits until there is something real to point at.

02 — Example, hospitality

Three Rooms

Several openings, one story.

The brief is a group about to market each room as a separate brand. The work is one line, three chapters, a stills brief, and the same script at the door as in the film.

03 — Example, public interest

Field Report

A report written so people can finish it.

The brief is an organization with data and no appetite for a stunt. The work is a short set of essays, a press briefing for the people who did the research, and a pitch that is a story.

04 — Example, fashion

After the Drop

A reason to come back, for the shop floor.

The brief is a label that does not need another drop. The work is a membership story, a calendar the team can run, and a talk track for the floor.
