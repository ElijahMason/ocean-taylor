Selected work

Notes from the desk.

Four shapes the work takes. Each one starts with a sentence the brand can repeat, then the media that carries it.

01 — Consumer audio

Quiet Signal

A launch that let the reviews speak first.

The brand had a good object and a loud plan. I cut the plan. We wrote a short narrative, a press kit with three claims and proof for each, and a six-week social cadence that answered questions instead of announcing them.

The founder took eight conversations. Paid media started after the reviews, aimed at the lines reviewers had already used.

02 — Hospitality

Three Rooms

Three openings, one season, one story.

A group was about to market each room as a separate brand. I gave them one line and three chapters. Local partners carried the middle chapter. A stills brief let the photographer finish in a day.

The host stand got the same script as the film. Guests heard one story from the invite to the door.

03 — Public interest

Field Report

An annual report people actually finished.

The organization had data and no appetite for a stunt. We turned the report into four essays, trained the scientists for press, and pitched editors a specific story rather than a brand announcement.

The scorecard was completion and citation. A spike in mentions was a side effect, not the job.

04 — Fashion

After the Drop

Repeat purchase, told on the shop floor.

The label did not need another drop. It needed a reason to come back. I wrote the membership story, a content calendar the internal team could run, and a talk track for the people on the floor.

Paid media got a smaller job: point at the story, then get out of the way.
